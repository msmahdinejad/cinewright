# Review 2 — craft & detail (looked at: full-size stills at 5.2 s (speakers) and 12.8 s (call to action), the colour order of the wipes, the text contrast)

Typography follows the style bible (Space Grotesk for headlines, Inter for labels, no letter-spaced words except the kicker). Findings, ranked:

1. **Glow on the closing text (12.8 s): the bloom pass softens "Be in the room" and the pill label.** Cause: dark themes use bloom .3 at threshold .8, and white text on a dark gradient crosses the threshold. Change: bloom .3 → .22 in the template's grade so the type stays crisp while the glows around the pill remain.
2. **Pill colours (5.2 s): "Day 2" is lime with dark text, "Day 1/3" violet and pink with white — good contrast, but all three read as one family only because they repeat the palette.** Kept; the text colour is chosen automatically from the pill's luminance, so a theme change cannot make a label unreadable.
3. **Row separation (5.2 s): the rows are translucent cards on a dark grid.** Checked at full size: the dark-theme card shadow (54 px blur, 22 px offset) and each row's own coloured icon disc keep the three rows apart — separated by colour, not by edge. Accepted, no change.
4. **Wipe order:** stripes → circle → flood → stripes: four different shapes in four cuts (the craft gate asks for ≥ 4 transitions in a longer film; here every cut is different).
5. **Detail in the last third:** confetti uses the same four accents; the handle pill "breathes" (a 40-px ring pulse) so the final image stays alive for its 1.8-s hold.
