Typography follows the style bible: Space Grotesk for names and numbers, Inter for captions, one kicker with letter-spacing (the title's "SAVE THE DATE"), nothing else tracked. Findings, ranked:

1. **Ghost copy of the name (3.3 s, 4.7 s):** a 7.5 %-opacity copy of the speaker's name, 2.1× the size, drifts left at 55 px/s behind the real one. At full size it reads as a depth layer and never competes with the type; the one place it touches the disc (3.3 s, behind the mic) is tone-on-tone. Kept.
2. **Caption contrast:** the "Designing in the open · Day 1" line is white at 88 % on violet (≈ 4.9:1); on the cyan and lime floods the kit switches the caption to dark ink automatically (checked at 4.7 s, ≈ 9:1). A theme change cannot make a label unreadable.
3. **Carried disc (3.3 s, 4.7 s):** gradient, soft shadow and a gloss arc; the icon strokes follow the luminance of the fill (dark on the lime-cyan mic disc, white on the violet code disc). At 4.7 s the violet disc on the cyan flood keeps a clear edge — no change.
4. **Call to action (13.9 s):** the stack (headline at 26 % of the height, pill at 56 %, sub-line, button at 84 %) leaves a 200-px gap between the headline and the pill — the confetti lands there at 12.3–13.8 s, after which it reads as air for the final 1.2 s. Accepted; the pill's top-edge gloss is a thin line (the kit draws an arc only while the shape is round), and "framesummit.io" fits the pill with about 3 % padding on each side, verified unclipped at full size.
5. **Detail in the last third:** confetti uses the four accents; the handle pill breathes with a 40-px ring pulse; the button pops 1.35 s after the scene starts, so the last image is complete 1.5 s before the fade.
