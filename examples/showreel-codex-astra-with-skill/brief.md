# FORM / IN MOTION

> Make a dynamic 15-second motion graphics video, like a motion designer resume showreel. Go all out.

Deliver exactly 15 seconds, 1920 x 1080, 60 fps, H.264 + AAC. All graphics and sound synthesized from code; no external media. Final: ./final.mp4.

## Promise
A ring escapes its grid, becomes sculpture, typography, optical rhythm and a particle universe, then resolves into a signature. Visual verb: re-form. Hero at 11 seconds; final composition holds after 13.6 seconds.

## Style bible
- Palette: ink #111217, paper #F0EDE4, vermilion #FF4E28, violet #B8A5FF, acid #DFFE67.
- Fonts: Anton and Space Grotesk. Huge editorial type, no invented personal credentials.
- Motion: overshoot entrances, exponential exits, orbiting camera, quarter-beat edits; circular geometry connects shots.
- Grade: light grain, controlled bloom, chromatic aberration only on impacts.
- Audio: original 128 BPM F minor electro, metallic plucks, tactile edits, brief silence before the hero, resolved chord.
- Never: generic neon wallpaper, static slides, placeholders, invented client work.
