# FRAME / Motion with intent

> "make a dynamic 15-second motion graphics video that shows what an incredible motion designer you are, like it's your showreel for a résumé. Go all out."

## Promise
An electric arc evolves from raw pulse into a finished signature, proving range across kinetic typography, glossy 3D, particle choreography, generative graphics, interaction design, shader art, and original sound in exactly 15 seconds.

## Visual verb
**Compose**: bend, multiply, shade, explode, and finally lock the same circular signal into a motion-design identity. The work is the résumé; the film invents no person, company, projects, or client claims.

## Audience / format
Portfolio and résumé viewers. English. 16:9, 1920×1080, 30 fps, exactly 15 seconds. H.264 video with stereo AAC sound. All artwork and music are generated locally in code; no stock media, downloaded assets, AI imagery, or AI video.

## Style bible
- Palette: ink #080A12; deep cobalt #162A6B; cyan #22E6FF; ultraviolet #7B5CFF; paper #F4F7FF.
- Fonts: Anton via K.FONTS.display for headlines; Space Grotesk via K.FONTS.grotesk for support; JetBrains Mono via K.FONTS.mono for technical labels.
- Motion: sharp eased slams with overshoot; camera drift in every scene; +2.4% hit punches; recurring arcs provide match-cut continuity; shot lengths accelerate toward the peak.
- Grade: dark analytic studio light, white/cyan highlights, violet separation, restrained bloom, low chromatic aberration, subtle grain and vignette. Paper/cyan poster scenes supply contrast.
- Sound: original 128 BPM electro score; saw bass, plucked arpeggio, house drums, panned whooshes, click/tick accents, an 80 ms pre-peak breath, layered impact, A-major add9 resolve. Target -14 LUFS and -1.5 dBFS ceiling.
- Never: random colors; static title slides; unearned résumé credentials; generic stock-looking backdrops.
