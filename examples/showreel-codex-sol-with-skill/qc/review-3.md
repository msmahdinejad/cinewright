Looked at: the revised waveform and spectrogram, the audio QC report, the final visual contact sheet, and the peak filmstrip from 11.60–12.00 s.

1. **0–12 s mix → sub-bass dominated the first score.** Cause: the original A1 roots and heavy kick/impact stack consumed headroom that small speakers would not reproduce. Fix: move the groove roots up an octave, reduce sub send and bass velocity, soften early hits, and preserve the low end only for the hero impact.
2. **11.55–11.72 s peak → no audible breath before the flash.** Cause: the riser and drums ran directly into the impact, reducing the perceived scale of the visual cut. Fix: duck music, drums, SFX, reverb, and delay into an 80 ms pre-peak gap, then restore the impact, kick, sub, and sparkle on the shared T.burst cue.
3. **13.125–15 s resolve → ending felt compressed.** Cause: the previous master stayed too dense after the peak. Fix: use a lighter glue ratio, let the A-major add9 pad and bells breathe, keep the tail exactly inside the 15-second duration, and confirm -14 LUFS / -1.5 dBFS headroom.

Result: audio QC passes at -14.1 LUFS and -1.4 dBFS true peak with reasonable band balance, no unintended silent gaps, and a visible dynamic rise into the peak.
