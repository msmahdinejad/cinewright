# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

## Our pledge
We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, language (**Persian, English and every other language are welcome in issues and discussions**), or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour
- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback — critique the work, not the person.
- Credit other people's ideas and work.
- Show your own work: bug reports with a reproduction, benchmark results with the raw files.

## Unacceptable behaviour
- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, and presenting AI-generated results as human-made or as measurements you did not run.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate. Report problems privately through GitHub (*Security → Report a vulnerability* works for this too) or to the maintainer's e-mail on their GitHub profile. All reports are handled confidentially.

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public. Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (CC BY 4.0).
