# {{TITLE}} — creative direction (CINEMA starter)

**Concept (one transformation):** smoke → a name → a world → a burst → a mark. The film is a journey in which one brand turns from vapour into a logo, passing through
chrome, type, a city and interface space.  *Replace this with your own transformation — A becomes B — in one sentence.*
**Audience & platform:** 16:9 for the web, 9:16 recomposition for Reels/TikTok (`--aspects 16:9,9:16`).  **Format:** 1080p · 30 fps · 20 s.
**Hero moment (≈ 75 %):** the burst at 15 s — silence, riser, impact, particles re-forming into the logo.  **Last image:** the mark + wordmark + tagline, held 2 s.

## Look
Palette: ink #05060f · aqua #7cf2ff · magenta #ff4fa3 · acid #d9ff1f · warm #ffd36b · paper #f2ede4.  Type: Inter 800 / Vazirmatn 800 (display), 500 (support).
Texture: grain + bloom + chromatic fringe on hits.  Motion: out-expo entrances, shock/punch on every beat, camera never still.
